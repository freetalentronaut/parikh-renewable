FAQ

Quick answers about installations & manufacturing

Typical rooftop timeline?

Residential installs: 3–7 days (site dependent). Commercial systems depend on approvals but we provide a project timeline during design.

Do you handle net-metering?

Yes — we prepare and submit applications to DISCOMs and coordinate the inspection & commissioning process.

Warranties?

Workmanship warranty (typical): 5 years; vendor product warranties per manufacturers.

Bulk DCDB / KCDB orders?

Yes. We manufacture and can ship OEM-labeled batches — contact sales for MOQ and lead times.
